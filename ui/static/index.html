Deal Flow

How a deal moves through Cover

Every stage is tracked in a single conversation thread inside the app. Nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Create a deal — escrow locks instantly

Open the app, tap New Deal. Enter the item, amount, and seller. A deal reference is generated. Pay using any saved method — Easypaisa, NayaPay, Sadapay, bank transfer, or USDT. Funds go into escrow, not to the seller.

2

Seller confirms and ships

The seller receives a notification and confirms the order inside the deal chat. They upload dispatch proof and the courier tracking number. Cover verifies the proof before marking the deal as in transit.

3

Package arrives — you inspect

Record an unboxing video before opening the package. Check the item matches what was agreed. The Cover app shows the expected delivery window and sends a reminder when delivery is due.

4

Any problem — raise a dispute inline

Wrong item, damage, or not as described? Tap Raise a dispute in the deal chat. Select the issue type, describe it, and attach evidence. Escrow freezes immediately. Cover reviews within 3 to 5 business days.

Confirm delivery — seller is paid

Everything correct? Tap Confirm delivery in the action card inside the chat. Payment releases to the seller instantly. Your trust score increases. The deal is archived with a full timestamped record.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I start a deal?
Open Cover, tap New Deal, enter the item name, amount, and the seller's phone number or username. The app generates a deal reference and locks the escrow. Pay using any saved payment method — the seller receives nothing until you confirm.
How do I confirm that I received my item?
Inside the deal conversation, a Cover action card appears once the seller marks the order as shipped. Tap Confirm delivery on that card. Payment releases to the seller immediately and the deal is closed.
How will I know what is happening with my deal?
Every stage appears as a message or card inside the deal conversation — payment received, seller confirmed, dispatched with tracking number, delivery expected. No separate WhatsApp chasing required.
Can I use Cover with a seller not on the app?
Yes. You can create a protected deal with anyone — a seller from OLX, Facebook Marketplace, or Instagram. Enter their details in the New Deal form. Both parties interact through the Cover deal conversation.
What payment methods are supported?
Easypaisa, NayaPay, Sadapay, bank transfer via IBAN, and USDT on Polygon. Add and save your preferred methods in the Payment Methods section of your profile. The seller sets their own payout method separately.
What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
Do not confirm delivery. In the deal chat, tap Raise a dispute on the action card. Choose the issue type, describe it, and attach your unboxing video and photos as evidence. Escrow freezes immediately. Cover reviews within 3 to 5 business days and issues a binding verdict.
Is an unboxing video required?
Yes. Record a continuous unboxing video before opening any package. This is your primary evidence for any dispute. Claims submitted without an unboxing video may not be upheld regardless of the issue.
Where does my money go during escrow?
Into a dedicated Cover escrow account, completely separate from operating funds. It is not invested or used. The full amount is held until you confirm delivery or a dispute is resolved. You can request a balance proof at any time by contacting support.
How do I list my shop as a seller?
Complete KYC in your profile — CNIC front and back, plus a live video verification call with Cover's team. Once approved, go to Profile, tap New Shop, and fill in your shop details. Your shop appears in the Shops section immediately.
Who pays delivery charges in a dispute?
If the item is correct but you changed your mind, you pay return shipping. If the item is wrong, damaged, or counterfeit, the seller pays return shipping. If the seller never shipped, you receive a full refund including Cover's fee.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

When you create a deal or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), transaction details, product descriptions, and deal references. We also collect device information and IP address for security purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and verify your protected deals
  • To send deal status updates inside the app
  • To verify seller identity and prevent fraud
  • To resolve disputes between buyers and sellers
  • To improve our service and security measures

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share limited deal-related information with the counterparty involved in the same transaction to facilitate the deal. We may disclose data if required by Pakistani law.

Data Security

All deal information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized Cover team members. Payment hold details are kept in separate, restricted-access systems. CNIC and KYC documents are encrypted at rest using AES-256.

Data Retention

We retain deal records for a minimum of 12 months after deal completion for dispute resolution purposes. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us at help@cover.mom, subject to legal retention requirements.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us via the app support chat or email.

Return / Refund / Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Return Policy

You may initiate a return if the product received does not match the description agreed upon at the time of deal creation. Returns must be reported within 24 hours of receiving the product by raising a dispute inside the deal conversation. You must provide an unboxing video as evidence.

Refund Policy

  • Wrong item received: Full refund of the held amount. Cover's protection fee is non-refundable.
  • Damaged or defective item: Full refund after verification of unboxing video evidence.
  • Seller fails to ship: Full refund including Cover's protection fee.
  • Buyer cancels after payment is held: Refund subject to Cover's review. If the seller has already incurred costs, a partial refund may apply.
  • Dispute resolved in seller's favour: Payment released to seller; no refund to buyer.

Refunds are processed within 3 to 5 business days after dispute resolution, returned via the same payment method used.

Shipping Policy

  • Delivery charges: The buyer is responsible for delivery charges unless otherwise agreed in the deal terms.
  • Courier selection: Cover recommends tracked courier services (TCS, Leopards) for all shipments.
  • Shipping timeline: The seller must ship within the timeframe specified in the deal, or within 3 business days if no timeframe was set.
  • Return shipping (buyer changed mind): Buyer bears the return shipping cost.
  • Return shipping (seller fault): Seller bears the return shipping cost.

Unboxing Video Requirement

All buyers must record an unboxing video showing the package condition, product, and any defects. This video is mandatory for any return or refund claim. Claims without an unboxing video will not be accepted.
