Policies & Terms
Privacy Policy
Cover collects only the information needed to provide the escrow service: your name, contact details, and CNIC for verification. We never sell your data to advertisers. Payment information is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256. You can request account deletion at any time by contacting support.

We may share information with our banking partners to process payments, and with law enforcement if legally required. All analytics data is anonymised before processing.
Refund Policy
If a dispute is resolved in your favour, your escrow funds will be returned within 2–3 business days. Cover's service fee is non-refundable once a deal is created. If you cancel a deal before the seller ships, your funds will be returned minus the payment gateway processing fee (typically 1.5%).
Terms & Conditions
By using Cover, you agree to use the platform only for lawful transactions. You must not use Cover to trade prohibited goods including weapons, controlled substances, counterfeit items, or stolen property.

Cover reserves the right to freeze funds if fraud is suspected and to report suspicious activity to relevant authorities. Both parties agree that Cover's dispute decisions are final and binding.

Cover is currently in private beta and features may change without notice.
